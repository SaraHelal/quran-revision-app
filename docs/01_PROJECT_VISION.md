# Project Vision

## Project Name

Quran Revision

---

## Mission

To help Quran memorizers maintain consistent and organized revision through a simple, beautiful, and intelligent application.

---

## Problem Statement

This project was born from a real problem that I personally experienced while memorizing and reviewing the Holy Quran.

As my memorization grew, it became increasingly difficult to remember which surahs needed more attention. Sometimes I would notice that a surah had started to weaken, but I couldn't remember when I had last reviewed it or which surahs were becoming weak. As a result, my revision became random instead of being organized and consistent.

I searched for existing applications that could help me manage my Quran revision in a simple and effective way, but I couldn't find one that matched the workflow I was looking for.

That inspired me to build this application.

My goal is not only to solve my own problem, but also to help every Quran memorizer maintain a consistent and organized revision schedule, making it easier to preserve what they have memorized over time.

---

## Long-term Vision

To become a smart Quran revision companion that grows with the user throughout their memorization journey.
